The story so far

You're a marine, one of earth's toughest, trained for action and hardened in combat.
^
Three years ago, you punched a superior officer for ordering his men to fire on civilians.
^
He and his body cast were shipped to pearl harbor, while you were shipped off to mars.
^
Mars, home of the union aerospace corporation.
^
The uac is a multi-planetary conglomerate with radioactive waste facilities on mars and its two moons, phobos and deimos.
^
With no action for fifty million miles, your day consisted of sucking dust and watching restricted flicks in the rec room.
^
The military, uac's biggest customer, has been using the moons' facilities to research inter-dimensional space travel.
^
So far they've been able to open gateways between phobos and deimos, throwing gadgets into one and watching them come out the other.
^
Recently, however, the gateways have grown dangerously unstable.
^
"Volunteers" entering them have either disappeared or been stricken with a strange form of insanity***
^
babbling vulgarities, bludgeoning anything that breathes, before an untimely death: explosive dismemberment.
^
Matching heads with torsos to send back to the folks became a full time job.
^
Latest reports state that the research is encountering a "small setback," but that everything is under control.
^
A few hours ago, mars received a garbled message from phobos. "we need immediate support.
^
"Something fraggin' evil is coming out of the gateways! computer systems have gone haywire!"
^
The rest was incoherent. then deimos simply vanished from the sky. attempts to contact both moons have failed.
^
Your squad were sent up pronto to phobos. you were to secure the perimeter of the base while the rest of your team went inside.
^
Comms relayed guns firing, sergeants yelling orders, screams, bones cracking... then finally, silence.
^
It's up to you

Things aren't looking too good. you're stranded, and there's no easy way off this godforsaken rock.
^
The assault team emptied the armory, leaving you with nothing but a pistol and a pot to piss in.
^
If only you could lay hands on a plasma rifle or even a shotgun, you could take down a few hostiles on the way out.
^
Whatever butchered your squad deserves a couple of pellets in the head.
^
You check your helmet and exit the dropship. as you step into the base, animal growls echo through distant corridors.
^
They know you're here. there's no turning back now.
